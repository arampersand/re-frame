Set up in 2 minutes

Free app. The AI runs on your ChatGPT account; classes transcribe on your Mac or with Groq.

Download and drag to Applications

Mac: pick Apple Silicon (M1–M4) or Intel (Apple menu → About This Mac). Windows: run the installer.

Open it the first time

We're not Apple-notarized yet, so macOS asks. Right-click the app → Open. On newer macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Click “Connect with ChatGPT”

Sign in with your ChatGPT account in the browser and you're in. The AI comes built in, nothing else to install.

Pick how to transcribe your classes

On your Mac: free and private, a one-time download from Integrations. Or Groq in the cloud with a free key from console.groq.com.
